Size guide

Every piece is made to order, so fit begins before you buy. Measure yourself, compare against the body measurements below, and check the garment measurements if you want to know exactly what arrives.

fit notes.

Measure first.

How to measure.

Use a soft tape measure against your body, not over clothing. For all horizontal measurements, keep the tape parallel to the ground and snug without compressing.

Waist. The narrowest part of the waist, usually just above the navel.

Hips. The fullest part of the hips and seat, feet together.

Chest. The fullest part of the chest, tape passing under the armpits and across the shoulder blades.

Underbust. Directly beneath the bust, where a band would sit.

Between sizes.

Leggings are compression fit. The flat waist measures five to seven inches smaller than the body it is made for, and stretches to meet you. If you fall between two sizes, order the smaller one.

The bra is a fixed band. Support comes from the band sitting level and staying put, not from tension. If you fall between two sizes, order the larger one.

These are different instructions because these are different garments. Buying a set does not mean buying the same letter twice.

Made by hand.

Each piece is cut and sewn to order. Finished measurements can vary by up to one inch.
